### Known limitations (from the README's "Scope and limits")

- A reference implementation, not a certification or a claim of universal
  safety.
- The OTLP importer supports an explicit subset of semantic conventions.
- The hash chain detects alteration of retained records; it does not prove
  completeness.
- Consequential deployments still need domain cases, repeated trials,
  calibrated human review, external anchoring, production identity and secrets,
  monitoring, and independent security assessment.

delivery metrics, real-user outcomes, and independent maintainability. No
